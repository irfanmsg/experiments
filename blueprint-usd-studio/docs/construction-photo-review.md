## What these images support

Photos 04 and 09 provide the clearest detail. The coarse polygon used for the current inferred main balcony can be presented more smoothly while retaining its agreement dimensions and tracing the interpolation. These photographs cannot supply an exact balcony radius.

A single-floor cutaway omits surrounding slabs and facade structure. That is a presentation limitation, not evidence that the measured apartment dimensions are wrong. Exterior or interior views intended to resemble the building need appropriate overhead/context detail; its height, thickness and structural positions remain unmeasured here.

The sales-model photographs can guide broader site presentation once the model is matched to the approved site plan. They are marketing/model evidence. The photographs do not establish that the landscaping, pool, courts or depicted building heights have been constructed as shown.

## What they do not establish

There is no interior photograph, ruler, surveyed reference, camera calibration or verified room-to-photo mapping. The images therefore do not establish room measurements, door locations, missing bathroom access, wall/slab thickness, storey height, furniture scale or apartment finish colors. Keep the agreement drawing and Annexure F finish schedule as their existing source authorities.

Green mesh, chutes, hoists and projecting catch nets are temporary construction features. Bare concrete does not specify the finished facade or apartment palette. A visible concrete balcony band could be structural edge detail or unfinished guard construction; these views do not justify replacing the agreement-specified glass railing with a solid concrete railing.

All 11 files have **empty EXIF**. No camera, lens, GPS location or capture date is available. The WhatsApp filenames contain a supplied date/time label, which is not verified capture metadata. All 11 SHA-256 hashes are unique; repeated views of the same facade still do not create independent metric measurements.
